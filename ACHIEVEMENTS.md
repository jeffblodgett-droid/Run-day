# Run-Day achievements

Leo's Run-Day badges: **100 for each activity mode** (500) plus **28 combo badges** (528 total). Each one unlocks from activities saved on this phone. The medallion ring shows the tier (bronze, silver, gold, diamond, legend) and the center color shows the mode. Locked medals turn grey.

Distance badges start close together (1, 3, 6, and 10 miles, with steps in between) and then spread out. Biking and one-wheeling go farther than running and walking.
